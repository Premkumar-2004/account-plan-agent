## Why I Built This

I was sick of wasting 2–4 hours researching one account just to get conflicting numbers and then writing the plan manually.  
So I built the tool I actually wanted:  
Say “Vercel” while walking → come back with a full account plan in my AirPods.

This is now the most valuable software I own.

---

## What You Get in 60–90 Seconds

- Correct official website
- Latest funding, revenue, valuation, headcount
- Real customers & competitors
- Current initiatives + 2025–2026 buying triggers
- Top 3 pain points with proof
- Decision-makers (when known)
- 3 killer messaging angles
- Full 90-day outreach sequence
- Every objection + how to crush it

In **voice mode** → the entire plan is read aloud to you.
